Week 1: We Love Teddy Bears

Learning Objective: Students will identify details about teddy bear museums and toys by listening, reading, and answering comprehension questions.

Competency: Applies reading comprehension strategies for short texts in English.

Key Skills: Critical Thinking, Teamwork, Communication.

Part I) Warm-Up & Vocabulary Review (~5 minutes)

Vocabulary review from Units 6 & 7, brain spark & Leader in Me habit connection.

Vocabulary Review (Units 6 & 7)

Review toys and sculpture words: teddy bear, kite, drum, ball, doll, robot, head, arms, legs, eyes, ears.

Quick Game:

"Flash Gesture Game": Teacher says a word (e.g., "robot"), students strike a robot pose instantly!

Leader in Me Habit Connection

Habit 3: Put First Things First. Organize your materials and attention before reading begins. Focus on listening attentively.

Guatemala CNB Context:

Connecting cultural appreciation of toys and objects with global themes in English.

Part II) Individual Work (20 minutes)

Student Book & Workbook exercises with self-check solution drawers.

Reading Task: Student Book Page 104-105

Read "We Love Teddy Bears". Circle Yes or No for the statements on page 105 (e.g., "There are teddy bear museums: yes/no"). Answer counting questions: "How many teddy bears are white?"

Exercise 2 Answers: 1. yes | 2. no | 3. yes

Part III) Cooperative Work / Teamwork (30 minutes)

Pair work, role assignment, and tangible product creation.

Collaborative Mission

Work in pairs. Partner A asks: "What are your favorite toys?" Partner B answers using the dialogue bubble (e.g., "My teddy bear and my drum."). Switch roles.

Assigned Roles:

  • Speaker 1: Interviewer
  • Speaker 2: Presenter
  • Both: Note-takers & Artists

Leader in Me & Value Integration

Habit 4: Think Win-Win and unit values (e.g., Share your toys / Be clean). Partners listen with empathy and respect each other's ideas.

Tangible Product:

A mini partner dialogue card or drawing to share in class.

Part IV) Plenary & Home Investigation (15 minutes)

Mini-presentations, interactive trivia, evaluation rubric, and home investigation mission.

Interactive Trivia / Pasapalabra

Teacher leads a fast-paced vocabulary trivia game using flashcards. Teams earn points for correct pronunciation and sentence structure.

Take-Home Investigation Activity

Home Mission: Ask a family member what their favorite toy was when they were a child. Draw it and bring it to share next class!

Plenary Group Evaluation Checklist

Evaluation Criteria (1st Grade) Excellent (4) Good (3) In Progress (2)
1. Topic Mastery & Clear Explanation
2. Logic / Procedure Followed
3. Collaborative Work & Participation
4. Response to Plenary Questions
